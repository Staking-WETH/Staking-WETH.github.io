What is it

What staking WETH means

Staking WETH is not a single action on Ethereum. Consensus staking uses native ETH, and WETH is the ERC-20 wrapper of ETH that holds a fixed 1:1 value, so WETH has to become ETH somewhere along the way.

Two common routes

The first route is manual: unwrap WETH to ETH, then stake that ETH natively or deposit it into a liquid staking protocol. The second is to use a protocol that accepts WETH directly and does the unwrapping and staking inside its own contracts.

What you get back

Native staking leaves your ETH locked in the staking system under the protocol rules. Liquid staking returns a liquid staking token (LST) that represents your stake and can be held, moved or used elsewhere. Read the Ethereum staking overview for how consensus staking works.

WETH vs ETH

Staking from WETH or from ETH

The staking step itself is the same. What differs is the preparation, because WETH is a token and ETH is the native coin.

Which to pick

If you already hold ETH, stake it as is. If your funds sit in WETH, unwrapping first costs only gas and keeps the amount unchanged.

How to stake

How to stake WETH step by step

Before you start

Decide between native staking and liquid staking. Native staking has its own requirements and exit rules, while liquid staking is a smart contract deposit that returns an LST.

Steps

  1. Connect your own wallet and confirm it holds the WETH you plan to stake, plus a little ETH for gas.
  2. Unwrap WETH to ETH. This is a 1:1 conversion that costs only gas.
  3. Choose how to stake: natively, or through a liquid staking route that returns an LST.
  4. Review the amount, the contract you are interacting with and the estimated gas, then sign.
  5. After confirmation, check your wallet for the staked position or the LST you received.

If a protocol accepts WETH directly, the unwrap step happens inside it, but you still sign each transaction.

Fees & gas

What staking WETH costs

Gas

Unwrapping and staking are separate Ethereum transactions, and gas is always paid in native ETH. See how gas works.

Unwrapping

Turning WETH into ETH has no trading fee and no slippage, because it is a fixed 1:1 conversion rather than a market swap. You pay gas only.

Staking costs

Liquid staking protocols and staking services may take a fee from rewards, and an LST can trade at a slightly different price from ETH on the market. These costs are shown by the protocol you use, not by this interface.

Keep ETH for gas

Holding only WETH leaves you unable to pay for transactions. Keep some native ETH in the wallet.

Security

Risks and safety when staking

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. This interface prepares the steps. It never holds your funds or keys.

Risks by step

  • Unwrapping: low risk beyond gas, but verify the WETH contract address because fake WETH tokens exist.
  • Native staking: funds are locked under protocol rules, rewards are not guaranteed, and penalties for validator faults apply.
  • Liquid staking: smart contract bugs, an LST price that drifts from ETH, and dependence on the operators behind the protocol.
  • Approvals: grant only what you need and revoke allowances you no longer use.

Smart contracts can hold bugs, so risk is never zero, and staking is not a savings product.

FAQ

Staking WETH FAQ

Can you stake WETH directly?

Ethereum consensus staking uses native ETH, so WETH cannot be staked there as is. You either unwrap WETH to ETH first, or use a protocol that accepts WETH and unwraps it inside its own contracts.

Do I lose value when I unwrap WETH before staking?

No. Unwrapping is a fixed 1:1 conversion with no slippage and no trading fee. The only cost is the gas for the transaction, paid in ETH.

What is the difference between native and liquid staking of WETH?

Native staking puts ETH into the staking system under its rules, with no token returned. Liquid staking returns a liquid staking token that represents your position and can be held or moved, at the cost of added smart contract risk.

Do I need ETH if I only hold WETH?

Yes. Gas on Ethereum is paid in native ETH, so you need some ETH to unwrap, approve or stake. WETH cannot pay for gas.

Do I need to approve WETH before staking?

Unwrapping WETH does not need a separate approval. A protocol that takes WETH directly usually needs a one-time ERC-20 allowance first, which is an extra transaction with its own gas.

Are staking rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards depend on the staking system and, for liquid staking, on the protocol and any fee it takes. Validator penalties, smart contract bugs and LST price moves can all reduce what you end up with.
