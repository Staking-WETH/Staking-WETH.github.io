What is Staking WETH?

Lido is an Ethereum liquid staking protocol: WETH holders unwrap to native ETH, stake through the official app, and receive stETH as the liquid staking token. Ethereum.org describes WETH as an ERC-20 representation of ETH redeemable back to ETH.

How Staking WETH works

  1. Prepare WETHUse Ethereum mainnet WETH and keep native ETH available for gas.
  2. Unwrap firstConvert WETH back to ETH through a trusted wallet or WETH contract route, since Lido staking uses ETH as the input.
  3. Stake in LidoOpen stake.lido.fi, connect your wallet, enter the ETH amount, and review the transaction before signing.
  4. Receive stETHAfter the stake transaction settles, stETH appears in your wallet and can be held, used in DeFi, or withdrawn through Lido's withdrawal flow.

Before you stake

Built around ETH

The clean route is WETH to ETH to stETH; that keeps the action aligned with Ethereum's native staking asset instead of assuming a direct WETH deposit path.

Liquid staking output

Lido mints stETH for deposited ETH, so the position remains tokenized instead of requiring you to run validator infrastructure; read the Lido docs for protocol details.

Domain-first routing

Use stake.lido.fi for the app path, check wallet prompts carefully, and avoid lookalike domains before signing any transaction.

Staking WETH FAQ

Can I stake WETH directly with Lido?

Lido's staking widget is built for ETH deposits. For WETH, unwrap to ETH first, then stake ETH in the official app to receive stETH.

What does staking WETH mean in practice?

It usually means converting wrapped ether back to native ETH and then using a liquid staking protocol, because Ethereum validators stake ETH, not ERC-20 WETH.

Do I need ETH for gas when using WETH?

Yes. Ethereum gas is paid in native ETH, so keep some ETH in the wallet before unwrapping, staking, or withdrawing.

Where can I verify WETH before staking?

Use the Wrapped Ether guide and confirm the connected app domain before signing any transaction.

What risks matter before staking WETH?

Risks include smart contract risk, validator penalties, changing rewards, liquidity-token market price risk, and user mistakes such as signing on a spoofed domain.
